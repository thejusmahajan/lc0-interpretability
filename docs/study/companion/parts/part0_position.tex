\section{The Position Setup}

\begin{center}
\input{figures/fig_c_board.tex}
\captionof{figure}{\textbf{The 4-legal-move King+Pawn endgame position.} FEN: \texttt{4k3/8/4K3/4P3/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 1}. White King on e6, White Pawn on e5, Black King on e8. White to move.}
\label{fig:board}
\end{center}

White to move. Exactly 4 legal moves:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item \textbf{Kd6} --- step forward-left (keeps the win)
  \item \textbf{Kf6} --- step forward-right (keeps the win)
  \item \textbf{Kf5} --- step backward-right (throws the win away, draws)
  \item \textbf{Kd5} --- step backward-left (throws the win away, draws)
\end{itemize}

Four legal moves is the point. Every number in the search can be written on one line, so nothing has to be taken on trust.
